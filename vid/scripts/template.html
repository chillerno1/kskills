How Shazam works

From a noisy clip to a song title in seconds.

Sound becomes a picture

Time runs left to right. Pitch runs bottom to top.

Peaks become a fingerprint

Peak at 2.1s, 440Hz
+
Peak at 2.4s, 880Hz
= one hash

Thousands of hashes per song. Noise changes a few. Most survive.

Match against every song

Hashes from your clip are looked up in a database of millions of songs.

The one whose hashes line up in the same time order wins.

That's Shazam.
